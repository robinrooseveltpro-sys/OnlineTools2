What is a favicon?

A favicon is a small icon associated with a website. Browsers can display it in tabs, bookmarks, and other interface locations. Google may also use a website's favicon in eligible organic search results, subject to its published requirements and processing.

Which favicon size should you use?

Different platforms can use different icon sizes. This tool creates several common sizes from one design. Google recommends a square favicon larger than 48 × 48 pixels for better display across surfaces. Check your specific platform's requirements before publishing a production app.

What makes a good website icon?

Simple shapes, clear contrast, and minimal text often work better than detailed designs when the icon appears at a very small size. Use a logo you have permission to use, and preview it at 16 × 16 pixels before installing it on your site.

Frequently asked questions

Is this favicon generator free?

Yes. You can design an icon, generate the package, and download the assets without an account or paid export service.

Can I create a favicon without a logo?

Yes. Switch to Text or emoji mode to make a simple icon with letters, numbers, or an emoji. You can customize its background and text color.

How do I install the favicon?

Extract the files into the website's public root folder and add the generated HTML snippet inside your homepage's head section. Adjust file paths if you store the icons in a different folder.

Does a favicon guarantee a Google search icon?

No. Google may show a favicon when its guidelines are met, but display is not guaranteed. The homepage and icon must be crawlable, and Google may need time to recrawl and process changes.

Are my uploaded images sent to a server?

This page's generator code uses local browser image processing and does not send your source image to a server. Be mindful of browser extensions, shared-device privacy, and the way you store exported files.

Why might an icon look different in some browsers?

Browsers and operating systems can select different file formats and sizes, and may cache an old icon. Check the relevant icon file paths, refresh the page, and allow time for browser or search-result updates.
