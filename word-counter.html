Who Can Use This Tool?

Students can check assignment lengths, writers can track article word counts, bloggers can analyze content, and social media creators can review text before publishing.

The keyword analyzer helps identify frequently used words. It is a simple text analysis tool, not a complete SEO ranking checker or a substitute for reviewing content quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the word counter work?

It analyzes the text entered in the editor and updates the word, character, sentence, paragraph and line counts.

Does punctuation count as a word?

Punctuation by itself is not counted as a word. Words separated by whitespace are counted, including many words containing apostrophes or hyphens.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute. Actual reading speed varies by person and content difficulty.

Is my text stored on your server?

No. The counting and analysis features run locally in your browser. The page does not send your text to a server.

Can I download my text?

Yes. Use the Download TXT button to save the text in a plain-text file.
